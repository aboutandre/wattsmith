# v0.17.3 — stored batteries leave the fleet maths

**Fix.** On 13 Nov two of the three batteries (F10, F11) were unplugged and stored. The safety supervisor excluded them correctly, so only F9 got setpoints, but the planning maths kept counting them:
- **Adaptive fleet headroom** kept all three capacities, because Wattsmith remembers each battery's last capacity so a missed poll doesn't shrink the fleet. It never forgot one, so the headroom showed 3 × 5,120 Wh × (100 − SOC) and opened the charge ceiling too early.
- **SOC calibration** kept tracking the stored batteries. They can never reach 100 % while offline, so calibration stayed "due" forever and the ceiling was forced to 100 % every day (with a grid top-up when that switch is on).
- **Charge power** for the arbitrage planner and the default grid-import cap counted 3 × 2.5 kW.

**What changed**
- A battery counts toward the fleet while it is available and for 15 minutes after its last good read. After that it drops out of capacity, headroom, calibration, charge power and the import cap, and comes back as soon as it answers again.
- A battery whose device or config entry is disabled in Home Assistant is ignored entirely. Disable a battery's device to park it explicitly.

**What changes for you**
- With one battery online, **Wattsmith Adaptive Fleet Headroom** shows that battery's room only, and **Wattsmith SOC Calibration** lists only the batteries that are online.
