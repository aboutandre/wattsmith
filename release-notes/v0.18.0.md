# v0.18.0 — the plans in plain words

**SOC Calibration** and **Arbitrage Plan** now say what they will do, how much, when and why. They used to say things like `overdue — grid top-up waits for bucket 23 @ 28.7 ct`. A bucket is a 15-minute slot counted from now, which tells you nothing without doing the arithmetic.

Now, for the same situation:

> Calibration top-up: ~15 kWh at 12:15–14:30 (@ 28,7 ct). Overdue (drift 17,2 pts, 5,6 d since full).

**What each part means**
- **~15 kWh** is the grid energy the top-up is sized for. It is an estimate: it fills the fleet to 100 % from where it is now, and PV may cover part of it first.
- **12:15–14:30** are the exact times. If the cheapest slots are not back to back, each window is listed (`at 12:15–12:45 + 13:15–15:00`). A top-up that is already running says `until 14:30`, and a window on another day says `tomorrow`.
- **(@ 28,7 ct)** is the price. When the slots do not all cost the same it reads `(@ Ø 28,7 ct)`: **Ø** is the average. For the Arbitrage Plan the average is weighted by the energy bought in each slot.
- **Why** is in the sentence: `Overdue (drift …)` for a calibration, `to avoid buying at 39,1 ct from 20:00` for arbitrage.

**All the sentences**
- *Calibration:* `Calibration is off` · `No calibration needed: worst drift 7,2 of 8 pts` · `Calibration due (…). Charging to 100 % from PV; grid top-up from 12 pts or 7 d.` (or `grid top-up is off` / `no prices available for a grid top-up`) · `Calibration top-up: …` (planned) · `Calibration top-up running: … Not waiting for 16:45: only 1,2 ct cheaper.` (or `prices up to 39,0 ct come first`).
- *Arbitrage:* `Arbitrage top-up planned: ~4,8 kWh at 13:45–14:30 (@ Ø 27,9 ct) to avoid buying at 39,1 ct from 20:00.` · `Arbitrage top-up running: …` · `No top-up needed: PV and stored energy cover the forecast` · `No top-up worth it: no shortfall ahead is dearer than 18,0 ct, the break-even for the cheapest slot` · `No plan: prices or forecast missing`. If the plan charges above the usual Max Charge SOC it adds `Above the usual charge cap: saves ~4,3 ct more.`

**Number format follows Home Assistant's country**, not the interface language: in Germany `28,7 ct` and `Ø`, elsewhere `28.7 ct` and `avg`. Times are 24-hour local time. The sentences themselves are in English.

**New attributes for dashboards** on both sensors, so a card can use the numbers without parsing the sentence: `windows` (a list of `{start, end}`), `energy_kwh`, `price_ct` (average), `price_ct_min` and `price_ct_max`.

**What does not change**
- The sensor states (`ok`, `due`, `grid_waiting`, `grid_charging`, …) and every decision are exactly as before. Only the text and the extra attributes are new.
- The history DB keeps the previous technical wording in `arb_reason`, so existing analysis still works.
- If the wording ever fails, the sensor falls back to the technical text and the plan is unaffected.

Tests: 22 new ones for the wording (locale, windows across midnight, every calibration and arbitrage case, and the structured fields the planners now carry).
