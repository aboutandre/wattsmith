# v0.17.2 — no idle wait after a restart, and no 15-minute delay for a hundredth of a cent

Two small fixes that showed up together on 24 Oct, right after Home Assistant restarted with the batteries at 11 % and a cheap 13.8 ct window running.

**1. After a restart, the arbitrage plan no longer sits idle for five minutes.**
Right after Home Assistant starts, Tibber's price service can be missing or empty. The planner then had no prices, showed **Arbitrage Plan = `no forecast`** and did nothing, and the next try was a full five minutes away (and nothing was logged at the default level). Now, while a tick has no priced bucket, Wattsmith tries again after **15 s, then 30, 60, 120 and 240 s**, never later than the normal five minutes, and goes back to the normal interval as soon as prices are there. This also covers a Tibber outage and prices that do not reach the current quarter-hour.

The log now says what happens: one info line at the first miss with the reason, one warning if it lasts four ticks in a row (about two minutes), one info line when prices are back.

**2. A hundredth of a cent no longer postpones a grid charge.**
The planner buys in the cheapest quarter-hours first, and the current one only won an exact tie. On 24 Oct the current quarter-hour cost 13.81 ct and the next one 13.80 ct, so the planner gave the whole purchase to the next quarter-hour and the plan read "grid-charge 4 Wh". The batteries started charging a quarter-hour later, after the next re-plan, for a saving of 0.01 ct. A purchase at full power also needs a whole quarter-hour, so every deferral leaves less slack before the price rises.

The current quarter-hour now gets a head start of **0.25 ct/kWh** (about 0.6 ct on a 1.9 kWh purchase at most). A quarter-hour that is cheaper by more than that still wins, so real waiting is unchanged: a bucket 0.8 ct cheaper is still waited for. The profit check still uses the real price, and when the current quarter-hour just misses it the planner moves on to the later, cheaper ones instead of giving up.

**What changes for you**
- After a restart the plan is back on the first retry that finds prices (15 s, 45 s, 105 s … after the start) instead of up to five minutes.
- In a flat or jittery price stretch, a planned grid charge starts in the quarter-hour it is planned for.
- Nothing to configure.

The plan itself is still only recomputed every five minutes, so a new quarter-hour can start up to five minutes before the plan sees it. That is a separate change.

383 tests pass, including full coordinator ticks without prices and the 24 Oct case (13.81 ct now, 13.80 ct next).
