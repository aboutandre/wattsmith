# v0.17.2 — one corrupt battery reading no longer wrecks the load forecast

**Fix.** On 5 Nov at 18:20 one battery (F9) reported a grid power of 429,496,729,797 W for a single one-minute poll, a corrupt read from the Marstek. Wattsmith integrated it as a real discharge, so the history DB bucket for 18:15 holds about 7.2 GWh of "house load". The house-load forecast averages the last 8 weeks, so since then it has predicted about 65 MWh per quarter hour around 18:00 on every day, and about 193,000 kWh per day instead of about 7 kWh.

**What changed**
- Any power reading above 50 kW (battery, house, PV, grid or EV) is now treated as "no reading". It is not passed to the controller or written to the history DB.
- The load forecast and the discharge-overhead fit skip any history bucket above 12.5 kWh (50 kW for 15 minutes). The bad 5 Nov bucket stays in the DB but no longer counts.

**What changes for you**
- The forecast heals at the next re-fit (within 6 hours of the update). **Wattsmith House Load Forecast** should show roughly 7 kWh for today again, with no 18:00 spike.
