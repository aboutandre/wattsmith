# v0.15.1 — Adaptive Max SOC stops flipping between 80 % and 100 %

**Adaptive Effective Max SOC** decides whether the remaining sun may charge the batteries above the Max Charge SOC (80 %). It sometimes switched back and forth within minutes. Three causes are fixed:

- **Forecast wobble around the threshold.** Each 5-minute Solcast update moves the remaining-sun estimate by ~0.25 kWh. When that estimate sat right at the switching point (around noon on 25 Sep), the ceiling went 80 → 100 → 80 → 100 %. It still opens as before, but once open it only closes again when the surplus exceeds the room between 80 % and 100 % by **more than 1 kWh**.
- **Sunset.** At the moment of sunset, Home Assistant's sun entity already shows tomorrow's sunset time while it still reports "above horizon" for a few minutes. Wattsmith read that as a whole day of sun left and reopened the ceiling for ~4 minutes (24 Sep 19:19). It now recognises the sun is setting.
- **A battery missing one poll.** When a battery misses a reading, it briefly reports no capacity, and the fleet looked a third smaller for that minute. Wattsmith now remembers each battery's last capacity.

Nothing to configure. 302 tests pass.
