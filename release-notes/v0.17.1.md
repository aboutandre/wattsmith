# v0.17.1 — the discharge hold now actually holds

**Important fix.** Since July, the arbitrage **discharge hold** was calculated and shown on **Arbitrage Discharge Hold SOC**, but it never reached the batteries. The part of Wattsmith that sends battery commands used only the configured Minimum SOC and ignored the hold. So every decision to keep stored energy for a pricier hour was displayed but never carried out.

The night of 28/29 Sep shows it plainly. From midnight the planner wanted to keep everything for the 36–41 ct morning: the hold sat at the fleet's SOC all night (30 % at 02:00). But the batteries kept delivering about 110 W each through the 33–35 ct night and were empty when the 41 ct peak started at 07:00.

Now the batteries stop discharging at the hold. The house draws from the grid in the cheaper hours, and the stored energy is used in the pricier ones, as the planner intended.

**What changes for you**
- On nights like last night, the fleet will stay at the hold level through the cheaper stretch and discharge in the pricier morning or evening.
- Grid charging was not affected and works as before.

**Please watch the first evening and night** after updating, since this is the first time the hold acts on the batteries. **Arbitrage Discharge Hold SOC** shows where the fleet should stop discharging, and the fleet SOC should now stay at or above it.

343 tests pass, including a new test through the whole chain (arbitrage plan → manager → battery commands) that would have caught this.
